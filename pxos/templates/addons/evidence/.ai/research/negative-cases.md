# Negative Cases & Counterevidence Register
<!-- pxos:evidence:negative-cases v1.0.0 -->

This register documents anomalies, empirical failures, counterexamples, and cases where working hypotheses failed to replicate. Tracking negative cases is essential to prevent survivorship bias and define the exact boundary conditions of findings.
