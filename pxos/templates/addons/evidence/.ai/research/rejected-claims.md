# Rejected Claims Ledger
<!-- pxos:evidence:rejected-claims v1.0.0 -->

This ledger permanently records hypotheses and claims that were formally refuted during peer review, empirical testing, or adversarial audits. Recording rejected claims prevents teams and AI agents from cyclically re-investigating or reviving debunked assertions across subsequent sessions.
